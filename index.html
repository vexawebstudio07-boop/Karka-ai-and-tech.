Frequently Asked Questions
What is Pay After Placement?
You focus on learning without upfront costs. You only share your success (and the fees) after you land a tech job. Please confirm the current terms with the Karka team before enrolling.
Is placement guaranteed?
Nobody can strictly guarantee a job. With Pay After Placement, though, Karka is as invested in your career as you are, and works hard to make you job-ready.
Who are the mentors?
Industry veterans from top tech companies who keep the curriculum aligned with current industry standards.
Do I need a background in coding or CSE?
No. The curriculum takes you from curious beginner to confident professional, whether you’re from arts, science, or switching careers.
How long does the course take?
It varies by course, typically 6 to 8 months, including intensive training, project work, and internship periods.
Will I get internship or project experience?
Yes. A large part of the curriculum is live projects and simulations that mimic a real corporate environment.
